eBay Helper Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

eBay Helper is a private card-selling research and listing-analysis application operated for the owner's use. It helps identify collectible cards, review listing information, compare prices, and evaluate existing eBay listings. This page explains how information is handled when the application is connected to eBay.

Information we access

When the owner authorizes the application through eBay's official sign-in process, eBay Helper may access account identifiers, listing details, inventory, listing prices, status, and related performance information within the permissions granted. We do not request or store the owner's eBay password.

The application may also process photos of collectible cards and information entered by the owner. Card-identification services such as CardSight may receive card photos submitted for identification.

How information is used

Information is used only to help the owner manage and research collectible-card listings, generate draft listing text, evaluate sales trends, and compare potential pricing or listing strategies. Connecting the account does not itself change live eBay listings.

Storage and security

Application data and account-related records may be stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. OAuth credentials used to connect to eBay are stored in encrypted form. Private administrative access is password protected. Only the minimum account permissions necessary for available features are requested.

Sharing and service providers

eBay information is not sold to advertisers and is not provided for unrelated marketing. eBay and Cloudflare process information in connection with account authorization, APIs, hosting, and data storage. Card-identification providers may process images specifically submitted for identification.

Retention and deletion

Account-related information is retained only while necessary for the app's authorized functions, such as saved inventory history and listing analysis. An authorized user can disconnect the account and delete its saved OAuth credentials. Applicable account information will be deleted when required by verified eBay marketplace account-deletion notifications or a valid deletion request. Some limited operational records may be kept when necessary for security, compliance, or to document deletion processing, without retaining deleted account identifiers.

Your choices

Authorization can be revoked through eBay's third-party application access settings. To request access to or deletion of information saved by the application, contact the operator using the address below.
